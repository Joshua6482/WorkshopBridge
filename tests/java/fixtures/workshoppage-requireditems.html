Description
One of the most famous van series of all time, in part because it was a great workhorse and in part because of the A-Team tv show. It comes in regular variant and the A-Team package that has the optional spawns submod. They share most of the parts so you can install that crazy spoiler on your daily loot goblin van and spice things up while you steal everything from your undead neighbors.

Main features:
  • Front and rear bumpers with 2 tiered protection
  • Fully animated hood, trunk, doors and windows
  • Visible interior and character
  • All parts have 3d objects you can remove and place in the world
  • Extra parts to craft and find in the world to make the vehicle truly yours (bumpers, bullbars, spoiler, window armor etc)
  • Multiple texture variations
  • Armor protection for windows, doors, engine, headlights etc
  • 4 seats
  • You can use it in your current save game, just enable the mod in the load menu
  • It can tow and be towed
  • Not recolorable


If you can't wait to find this mod in your game and want to test it right away, use bikinitools to spawn it, hide the items that your character holds while in vehicle, or for all other good stuff that mod has, go get it!
